Who we are

We are an independent civic information and analysis platform focused on Haiti. We bring public sources together, explain institutions and examine candidates and public-policy questions. We are not affiliated with any political party.

Our mission

Make reliable civic and electoral information accessible so Haitians can understand public institutions, examine candidates and exercise their rights with informed, independent judgment.

Verify and document

Bring election and candidate information together with clear sources, dates and limits.

Explain public life

Provide understandable guides to rights, institutions and the electoral process.

Examine ideas and records

Publish evidence-based analysis and clearly labeled opinion so readers can form their own assessments.

Support informed participation

Help people find practical information to exercise their rights, ask questions and follow public decisions.

Our editorial commitments

  • The same standards for every candidate: independence requires scrutiny without preference for any party.
  • Separate verified information, interpretation and opinion. A CEP registry entry is not candidate approval.
  • Provide sources and dates; state what is unknown. A source we have not found does not prove that information or a document does not exist.
  • Accept corrections and new evidence, and date changes that affect an article’s meaning.
  • Describe our methods accurately. Do not present document analysis as interviews or field reporting we did not conduct.

From research project to civic platform

Ayiti Sitwayen began as a research and action project on civic engagement, social media and technology. Today the site includes civic education, election guides, candidate records, analysis and opinion. Research remains part of the project, helping us ask sound questions and work with evidence.
