Voter registration closes: May 20, 2027

To register on the electoral roll, follow the CEP procedure with your national identification card. The CEP has announced mobile teams and says you may choose your voting centre when registering.

Holding an identity card does not by itself confirm that you are on the electoral roll. Verify your registration with the CEP.

Practical questions

Does my identity card confirm that I am registered?

Holding an identity card does not by itself confirm that you are on the electoral roll. Verify your registration with the CEP.

Source: Conseil Électoral Provisoire (CEP)

Does the CEP centre directory confirm where I will vote?

The centre directory helps you find an address. It does not confirm an individual’s assigned voting centre.

Source: Conseil Électoral Provisoire (CEP)

Is everyone in the candidate statistics approved?

Candidate registration data are preliminary. Online registration and document filing are separate steps. The CEP’s validated list establishes official candidacies.

Source: Conseil Électoral Provisoire (CEP)

Can I already vote from abroad?

The CEP has announced preparations for a diaspora voting pilot. This does not mean overseas voting is already available everywhere. Await official information on the countries, centres, requirements and procedures that apply to you.

Source: Conseil Électoral Provisoire (CEP)
