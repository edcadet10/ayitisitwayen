Practical questions

Does my identity card confirm that I am registered?

Holding an identity card does not by itself confirm that you are on the electoral roll. Verify your registration with the CEP. Official source →

Does the CEP centre directory confirm where I will vote?

The centre directory helps you find an address. It does not confirm an individual’s assigned voting centre. Official source →

Is everyone in the candidate statistics approved?

Candidate registration data are preliminary. Online registration and document filing are separate steps. The CEP’s validated list establishes official candidacies. Official source →

Can I already vote from abroad?

The CEP has announced preparations for a diaspora voting pilot. This does not mean overseas voting is already available everywhere. Await official information on the countries, centres, requirements and procedures that apply to you. Official source →
