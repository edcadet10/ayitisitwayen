Claude Joseph, EDE and Smith Augustin: what responsibility does a party carry?

A party defended its representative, then sought his resignation. The sequence invites scrutiny of political decisions without transferring criminal allegations between people.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

The April 2024 agreement identified Smith Augustin as the RED/EDE and Compromis Historique representative on the Presidential Council. That documents the link between Claude Joseph’s EDE party and the nomination. Joseph was not himself a council member. [1]

On July 27, 2024, Gazette Haïti reported that EDE’s secretariat under Sterline Civil defended Augustin’s opportunity to answer the BNC allegations; he refused resignation. On September 6, Le Nouvelliste reported EDE’s national political bureau asking him to leave the council. These were distinct positions at different moments. [2] [3]

A separate judicial process followed. In the February 19, 2025 operative wording reproduced by RNDDH, the Court of Appeal directs continued investigation while rendering the summonses inoperative during the councillors’ status. That did not adjudicate guilt. This stage is not presented as the complete case status today. [4]

For Joseph as a candidate, the political issue is how his party selects representatives, examines warnings and acts when a representative is challenged. The nomination does not establish that Joseph solicited or received BNC money. Holding a party to account for its decisions is more sound than transferring another person’s allegations to all members.
