Wilner Joseph and the VIKTWA primary: two reports, two vote counts

Both reports name him the winner, but their figures differ. The minutes matter.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

Wilner Joseph won VIKTWA’s internal presidential selection in September 2026. But place two reports of the vote side by side and their numbers differ. The discrepancy merits clarification without inventing fraud. [1] [2]

Juno7 on September 10 reports 43 participants from the grouping’s claimed 52 parties: 39 votes for Joseph, three for Jean Nerva Siméon, and one blank. It attributes those figures to VIKTWA’s communiqué. [1]

FCN’s September 11 article gives Joseph 43 votes out of 49, with three for his rival. Neither the total nor the winner’s tally matches. We have not located the original minutes needed to resolve the discrepancy. [2]

Both versions describe a substantial Joseph victory. The difference is not proof he lost or that parties manipulated the vote. It shows why an internal result needs a record stronger than repeated news coverage.

Publishing selection rules, eligible structures, participation totals and signed results would make the process easier to examine. Support among party leaders does not measure support among the country’s voters: an internal primary is not a national poll.
