Munir Mourra: Jacmel’s energy plan already has a construction site to account for

MPH names Jacmel in diesel-plant rehabilitation. A World Bank report documents a separate solar-and-battery construction project there.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-16

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

A new government does not inherit a blank map. It inherits projects, contracts and equipment. Jacmel provides a concrete test of what Munir Joseph Mourra’s energy programme must connect before announcing a fresh start.

MPH proposes solar and wind farms over four years and rehabilitation of Péligre dam and diesel plants in Carrefour, Gonaïves, Cap-Haïtien and Jacmel. These are proposals from the party presenting Mourra as its candidate, not a list of verified funded contracts. [1] [2]

The World Bank’s October 26, 2026 P156719 report says Jacmel’s solar-and-battery facility was approximately 35% complete, with completion expected in April 2027. That is financier-reported construction progress, not 35% of the city’s electricity needs being met. [3]

The sources establish no conflict between the projects. Solar, batteries and diesel can have different roles within a service. Connecting them requires answers: which equipment works together, which contracts continue and who funds maintenance, fuel and battery replacement?

For residents, the question is hours of electricity and the bill. Mourra’s test is an inventory of committed projects and a service plan accounting for them. Explained continuity can be more useful than another opening ceremony.
