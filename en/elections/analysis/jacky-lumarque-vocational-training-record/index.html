Jacky Lumarque: the vocational-training test starts before the campaign

An education career supplies experience to examine, not results to assume.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

Before becoming rector, Jacky Lumarque worked in state vocational training. Université Quisqueya’s biography dates his INFP deputy-director appointment to 1984 and director-general appointment to 1987. It says he has been the university’s rector since 2007. [1]

That makes youth employment a question about his record as well. Beyond saying what he would do, he can show what institutions he led delivered. The biography comes from his own institution, not an independent performance evaluation.

A training centre can issue many certificates without everyone finding employment. Assessment requires entrants, completions, cost per participant and subsequent work in the trained field. The same questions should apply to every candidate promising training.

Another choice needs explanation: who pays? Families, employers and the state have different resources and interests. If a programme depends on student fees, the candidate should show how poorer young people gain access.

A candidate’s diploma will not pay a young person’s wage. A system connecting learning to available work may. Lumarque’s previous responsibilities give the public a specific starting point for examining that connection.
