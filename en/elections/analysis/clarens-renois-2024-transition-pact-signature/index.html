Clarens Renois: the signature tying him to transition promises

He was not a Presidential Council member. His signature on the 2024 agreement nevertheless gives voters a concrete commitment to examine.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

Page 13 of the April 2024 political agreement carries Clarens Renois’s signature for the Collective of Political Parties of January 30, 2023, alongside Liné Balthazar and Edgard Leblanc Fils. This is a documented connection to the transition’s design. [1]

The pact prioritized security, reform and elections. Article 12.1 set February 7, 2026 as the council’s end date; article 13 barred an extension. Article 47 envisaged a sectors’ assembly to monitor implementation and make recommendations. The text provided a mechanism for scrutiny as well as distributing roles. [1]

The collective’s council member was Edgard Leblanc Fils, not Renois. Decisions and failures cannot automatically be assigned to a nonmember. A signatory can nevertheless account for what he supported, what he challenged and how he used the proposed oversight mechanisms. [1]

This distinction matters at election time. Political accountability extends beyond spending authority, while remaining distinct from criminal liability. The pact establishes no offence by Renois. It supplies a concrete standard against which to examine his transition positions and actions: the commitments he signed.
