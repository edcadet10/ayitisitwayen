Martelly’s lobbying file: who is paying?

A U.S. filing reports $75,000 received; the contract specifies other amounts. Those are different facts.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

In a filing submitted in Washington on November 5, 2026, Liberty Government Affairs reports receiving $75,000 from Invisible Investigations LLC. The receipt is dated September 10. The foreign principal served by the work is Michel Joseph Martelly, through that intermediary company. [1]

The disclosure is in the FARA register, which makes certain agents’ relationships with foreign principals public. It establishes a disclosed service relationship, not a corruption charge or proof that DOJ independently audited every financial declaration. [1]

The attached letter is dated October 22 and signed October 26. It specifies a $125,000 deposit, a $50,000 monthly retainer and expenses normally capped at $5,000 monthly. It says it supersedes prior agreements. These terms must not be presented as amounts already paid. [2]

Another important detail: the agreement says Martelly must not directly or indirectly reimburse or compensate Invisible Investigations for these payments. It calls for advance payments using dollars already held at U.S. financial institutions. These are contractual conditions, not independent proof of the ultimate origin of all funds. [2]

The remaining question is specific: what financial relationships support the intermediary, and how does the $75,000 receipt relate to the newer contract terms? The filing reviewed does not supply enough information to answer everything. It does not justify inventing a hidden financier.
