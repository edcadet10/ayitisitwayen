Hans Joseph: a million dollars on paper, but the houses had not started

An ULCC document in the OAS archive shows what an image can reveal and what it cannot decide.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

On paper, a social-housing project had received one million U.S. dollars. In drone images of the site, house construction had not begun. That is how the ULCC described an investigation in its 2021 submission to the OAS anti-corruption mechanism. [1]

The document names Hans Jacques Ludwig Joseph as director general. It says the land’s dimensions did not match the project’s description. This belongs to the record of the institution he led; it does not establish that he personally operated the drone. [1]

The useful method is comparing what an administration pays for with what actually exists. A contract and receipt do not establish that a family has a home. Images, site visits, measurements and payment records should be checked against each other.

But this is the ULCC’s account of its own work. It does not provide a final judgment here or a complete accounting of money recovered. We do not turn an investigative allegation into a conviction. [1]

As a candidate, Joseph can explain how he would make this routine: verify delivery before final payment, preserve evidence, and follow justified cases through court and recovery. Finding a discrepancy starts the work; it does not complete the outcome.
