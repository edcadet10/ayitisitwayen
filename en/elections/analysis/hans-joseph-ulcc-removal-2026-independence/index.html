Hans Joseph: leaving ULCC and claiming independence

The former director offers an explanation for his dismissal. His account and a proven motive are different things.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

In a July 5, 2026 AyiboPost interview, Hans Ludwig Joseph discussed leaving ULCC, which he had led since 2020. AyiboPost reported that Goethie Varnelle Morency was installed July 3 to replace him. Joseph’s campaign record is therefore that of a former director. [1]

Joseph said his removal followed a Security Council presentation in which he criticized the High Court of Justice decree. He also said no working meetings had occurred with the Prime Minister’s Office since Alix Didier Fils-Aimé took office in November 2024. These are his statements to AyiboPost, not events Ayiti Sitwayen witnessed in a closed meeting. [1]

The sequence warrants asking whether his criticism played a role. Timing alone does not prove the dismissal’s motive. Establishing a direct connection requires decisions, communications or other evidence linking criticism and removal. Otherwise chronology becomes an unsupported conclusion about intent.

For voters, the larger independence test concerns what Joseph would guarantee others if elected. How freely could oversight bodies examine his allies? What public explanation would accompany leadership changes? A claim of independence becomes stronger when translated into rules protecting an institution across changes of leadership.
