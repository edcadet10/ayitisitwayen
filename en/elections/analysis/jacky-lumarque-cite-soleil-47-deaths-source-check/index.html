Jacky Lumarque’s 47 deaths a day: what the linked report actually counted

A July 2022 comparison loses a crucial distinction between deaths and injuries. The horror needs no inflated category.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-18

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

On July 17, 2022, Jacky Lumarque’s account compared 30 people killed daily in Ukraine with 47 killed daily in Cité Soleil from July 8 to 12. The Creole post asked whether Haiti was at war. Its visible French parent quoted UN News and linked a report. [1]

The linked July 16 report says at least 234 people were killed or injured over those five days. It also reports 99 deaths and 135 injuries. The five-day figure combines two different outcomes. [2]

Dividing 234 by five gives 46.8. That resembles the rounded 47 in the post. It cannot establish 47 deaths each day because the total includes injured people. The author’s actual calculation is unknown. [2]

The source for the Ukraine figure remains unidentified. A counting mistake or hurried summary is a plausible explanation; deliberate deception is not established. We have not established whether Lumarque later corrected or explained the comparison.

The UN report still describes targeted residents, sexual violence and blocked humanitarian access. Correcting the count does not diminish that suffering. It makes the public argument answerable to the people being counted. [2]
