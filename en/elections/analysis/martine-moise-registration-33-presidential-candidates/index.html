Martine Moïse joins the list: 33 presidential registrations

Three women appear in the late November 15 reading. Registration opens a record to examine; it is neither final approval nor a measure of election chances.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

Martine Moïse appears in the CEP presidential registry under GAD, with the full name Marie Etienne Martine Joseph Moise. Our late November 15 reading records 33 registrations. Reuters identifies the new registrant as the former first lady and Jovenel Moïse’s widow. This connects the registry’s full name with her public name without inventing another identity. [1] [2]

The count has changed: an earlier check recorded 32 names, with Dominique Dupuy and Savannah Savary as the two entries coded F. The new entry makes three of 33, or about 9.1 percent. This measures registry presence. It is not a poll, a vote forecast or a finding about a legal quota. [1] [3]

Registration does not mean the CEP has finished examining documents or approved the candidacy. GAD’s registry label supplies neither campaign financing, donors nor all alliances. It identifies the structure recorded with the candidate. These steps must stay separate so voters do not mistake a website entry for a final decision. [1]

A familiar name does not replace a programme. Examining the candidacy requires separating her previous role, documented decisions and current commitments. Asset declarations, funding sources, personnel and specific measures on security and living costs would let the public move beyond reputation. The same scrutiny must apply to every other candidate.
