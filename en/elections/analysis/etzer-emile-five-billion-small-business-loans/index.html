Etzer Emile: 5,000 small projects mean 5 billion gourdes

The arithmetic is simple. Choosing beneficiaries fairly is the harder test.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

One million gourdes could change what a small business can do. Etzer Emile proposes interest-free loans and a guarantee fund for 5,000 projects, at 1 million gourdes each. His campaign’s jobs page places the measure in its first 100 days. [1]

Multiplication gives 5 billion gourdes. That is the value of 5,000 loans if each receives that amount; it is not automatically the state’s total expenditure. Administration, guarantees and unpaid loans should appear separately. [1]

A guarantee fund can encourage a bank to lend when it fears losses. A guarantee is not free money: if a borrower fails to repay, someone bears the loss. The rules should specify the shares carried by bank, state and borrower.

Readers should be able to find selection criteria, projects by department, repayment terms and rules for political officials’ relatives. A list of names alone cannot show whether someone was selected for a project or for connections.

The campaign also proposes 50,000 temporary public-works jobs and 300,000 formal jobs by 2033. These are not three ways to count the same outcome. A loan is a tool, temporary work ends, and a lasting job needs customers and revenue. [1]
