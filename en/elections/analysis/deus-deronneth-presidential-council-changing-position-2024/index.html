Deronneth and the council: rejection, qualified support, congratulations

Three dated messages show a change in public positioning. They do not explain a private motive.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-18

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

On March 19, 2024, Deronneth’s account shared a petition opposing the proposed presidential council. The linked petition offered an alternative transition structure. Sharing it establishes a public association with that proposal, not authorship of every line or authenticated mandates for every named signatory. [1]

On April 25, his account said the council was neither a solution in itself nor the best solution, but called its formation a major achievement. It wished the body courage and success while stressing security, competence and local participation. [2]

In September, a published letter congratulated council president Edgard Leblanc Fils on his United Nations speech. The letter documents praise. It does not establish that the recipient received it, met Deronneth or entered an electoral agreement. [3]

The sequence complicates a simple label of permanent opposition or unconditional support. Pragmatic acceptance, a changed arrangement or continued critical cooperation could explain the shift. A secret payment or bargain is not established.

The question voters can fairly ask is what changed between March and April: the structure, the safeguards or his judgment? What conditions would make him withdraw support? The dates establish a change in public positioning. His explanation and documented actions would establish more.
