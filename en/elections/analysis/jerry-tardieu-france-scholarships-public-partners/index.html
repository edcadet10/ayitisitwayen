Jerry Tardieu and French scholarships: who financed the opportunity?

A deputy’s role in assembling partners deserves recognition. Institutional funding and work also need to remain visible.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

A December 14, 2016 account on Jerry Tardieu’s own website describes the Haiti–France excellence scholarship launch. It identifies BRH as financier of the first cohort and the parliamentary friendship groups as participants. It also records partners’ promise of merit-based selection. [1]

Assembling a partnership differs from personally financing every scholarship. A record can credit the deputy’s initiative while identifying who funds, selects and administers training. Otherwise collective work can disappear behind a political name.

Le National’s report on France’s 2020–2021 scholarship launch quotes Ambassador José Gomez discussing partners including BRH, Fondation Sogebank and AUF. Tardieu attended. The report covers several arrangements; it does not establish that all were the 2016 agreement or individually attributable to Tardieu. [2]

Evaluating outcomes requires selection criteria, funded scholarship totals and graduates’ subsequent work. Ceremony attendance does not answer those questions, and a promise of merit does not prove every selection was impartial. Those records can turn a diplomatic initiative into a substantiated public record.
