Before Martine Moïse’s entry: Dupuy and Savary in the 32-name list

The earlier November 15 reading had two entries coded F among 32. Martine Moïse’s later entry brought the list to 33, with three coded F. These are two points in a changing registry.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

Of 32 presidential registrations reconciled across four CEP data pages on November 15, 2026, two carry F in the sex field: Dominique Dupuy for PVD and Marie Anne Savannah Hadid Savary for HAMAD. Thirty carry M. Two divided by 32 is 6.25%. [1]

This is a time-specific snapshot while registrations could still change, not a final approved-candidate list. It does not mean women are 6.25% of voters or that only women support these candidates. The calculation counts the source field rather than inferring it from names. [1]

The figure opens an investigation into routes to presidential nomination: recruitment, funding and selection. It does not identify which barrier blocked which person. It documents an imbalance in participation that warrants examining those processes.

Dupuy and Savary merit the same scrutiny as other candidates: experience, decisions, economic interests and programmes. Their presence should not reduce them to symbols. Meaningful choice requires access to competition and consistent scrutiny of everyone entering it.

Update: in the late November 15 reading, Martine Moïse appears under GAD. We now verify 33 registrations, including three coded F. The two-of-32 figures above preserve the earlier reading; they are not the latest count. [2]
