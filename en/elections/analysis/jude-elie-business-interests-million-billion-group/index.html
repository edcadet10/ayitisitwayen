Jude Elie: business interests need a clear map

Two public pages identify starting points for examining private interests, not a complete asset declaration.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

Million Billion Group’s team page presents Jude Elie as a partner for infrastructure and development strategy. It still named him when downloaded November 15, 2026. This is a company-site statement, not a verified shareholder register. [1]

His own biography says he owns construction company EJDECO, GESA Green Energy and petroleum-distribution company SEPSA. The page supplies no publication date allowing us to treat these statements as an updated asset declaration. [2]

The public-interest point is the sectors. Infrastructure, energy and fuel involve public contracts, regulation or tax decisions. That does not establish that the candidate received favours; it identifies financial interests an assessment needs to map.

A sound map would separate ownership, advisory work and ended relationships, with dates, interests held and responsibilities. The word “partner” alone cannot tell us a person’s equity, money or decision-making power.

Neither company names nor appearing on the same website establishes campaign financing. That link requires contribution or payment records. Those records should anchor financial scrutiny.
