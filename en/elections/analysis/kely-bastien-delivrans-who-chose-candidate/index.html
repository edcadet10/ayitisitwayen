Kely Bastien: who selected him for DELIVRANS?

A coalition’s selection explains its internal organization, not the country’s voting intentions.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

Kely C. Bastien was not the only proposed DELIVRANS nominee. According to the communiqué reported by FCN, ANSANM proposed him and LÒD DEMOKRATIK proposed Djina Guillet Delatour. The platform’s presidents’ conference selected Bastien by majority on November 13, 2026. [1]

Delatour congratulated him and acknowledged the choice. In the statement reported by FCN, she retained reservations about the electoral process. Someone can accept an internal decision without abandoning every position of her own. [2]

For readers, three levels remain distinct: member parties propose, coalition leaders select, and citizens vote. A majority at the second level is not automatically a majority at the third. Public support requires separate measurement.

The next issue is what binds the groups: their common programme, how they decide on money and appointments, and what happens when they disagree. A coalition should explain its own rules before seeking to govern the country’s.

This concerns political organization, not an allegation of a secret deal. Naming each organization and decision stage provides a concrete starting point for examining those relationships.
