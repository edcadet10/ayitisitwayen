Jerry Tardieu vs Jacky Lumarque: a scholarship must identify who pays and who selects

Both attended a 2020 scholarship launch. That connection does not merge the programmes into one fund or make the financing a personal gift.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-16

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

For a student seeking a scholarship, the question extends beyond who appears in the ceremony photograph. Eligibility, selection and available funding matter. Jerry Tardieu’s and Jacky Lumarque’s records allow us to distinguish political connections, financing and selection.

On his own website, Tardieu described the December 2016 Haiti–France excellence scholarship launch. That account identifies BRH as financier of the first cohort and reports a commitment to merit-based selection. It supplies a partnership record, not proof that Tardieu personally financed the scholarships. [1]

Le National reports that Tardieu and Lumarque attended France’s 2020–2021 scholarship campaign launch. Ambassador José Gomez named BRH, Fondation Sogebank and AUF among partners. The article also announced a new Anténor Firmin doctoral scheme. Several mechanisms appeared at one ceremony; that does not make them all the 2016 agreement. [2]

Lumarque has a separate responsibility to examine: Université Quisqueya says he has been its rector since 2007. That does not turn every scholarship received by its students into his personal achievement. Similarly, a deputy’s initiative does not make him the owner of institutional resources funding a programme. [3]

The comparison requires a clear chain: funder, administrator, selection panel and beneficiaries. Published criteria and verifiable outcomes would let the public credit each contribution without creating a political debt for students. This record establishes no scholarship-for-votes exchange. It identifies the documents distinguishing an open opportunity from a benefit claimed as someone’s personal possession.
