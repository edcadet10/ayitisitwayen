Published comparisons

  • Savannah Savary vs Olivier Barrau: family businesses and the public interest

    Their business histories explain part of their careers. The public question concerns what they control today and the safeguards they accept for tomorrow.

  • Wilson Jeudy vs Jude Célestin: public works must account for the people affected

    Delmas camp clearances and CNE equipment purchases are different records. They offer two ways to examine authority, resources and public service.

  • Hans Joseph vs Lucmanne Delille: an investigation must reach a judge

    A former ULCC head and a former justice minister held different responsibilities. Their records direct attention to the full justice process, beyond an investigation’s announcement.

  • Jacky Lumarque vs René St-Fort: from the lesson to a student’s life

    Training experience and proposals on teachers’ conditions address different parts of the same chain. Access, learning and what follows all need examination.

  • Etzer Emile vs Munir Mourra: doubling police is not doubling budgets

    Two security proposals use doubling. One counts people; the other counts money. The outcome for citizens requires more than those figures.

  • Etzer Emile vs Moïse Durocher: what counts as a job?

    A small-business loan, temporary employment and a lasting job are different outcomes. Campaign totals need common definitions before comparison.

  • Clarens Renois vs Maxo Joseph: a church agreement does not own anyone’s vote

    They participated in the same political process in 2015. Later records show why alliances need dates and support networks must be distinguished from voters’ rights.

  • Jerry Tardieu vs Gary Bodeau: who controlled access in Parliament?

    The 2019 record raises access to documents. U.S. sanctions against Bodeau raise a separate question about money and influence that must remain distinct.

  • Dominique Dupuy vs Claude Joseph: after diplomatic words, what protection?

    The former foreign ministers bring different records at the same border. A public stance alone does not measure what changed for citizens.

  • Michel Martelly vs Jocelerme Privert: two presidents, different accountability tests

    Martelly’s school promise and Privert’s transitional mandate are different records. Comparing them requires separating money, authority and deadlines.

  • Jerry Tardieu vs Olivier Barrau: business experience must show whom it serves

    A hotel and an insurer received international financing. Comparing the records means following the money to the people it was intended to help.

  • Etzer Emile vs Jean Ernest Muscadin: more police, accountable to whom?

    One offers police recruitment targets. The other has a prosecutorial record that raises questions about the use of force. Security requires examining both.

  • Nestor, Savary and DPA: a launch did not settle every affiliation

    Dated records show why appearing at a political event does not, by itself, establish someone’s eventual party affiliation.

  • Célestin and Martelly in 2010: 234 tally sheets and a changed runoff

    The OAS report and criticism of its method show why recalculation differs from a full recount.

  • Martelly and Bodeau: why foreign sanctions do not automatically settle election eligibility

    Understanding their presence on the registration dashboard requires separating three different decisions.

How we work

Analysis examines what the evidence supports. Opinion makes a clearly labeled editorial argument. We distinguish verified information, our interpretations and open questions. The same sourcing and accuracy requirements apply to every candidate; the initial selection is neither a ranking nor an endorsement.

Ayiti Sitwayen takes responsibility for these publications. The cited sources let readers examine their basis and submit corrections or new evidence.
