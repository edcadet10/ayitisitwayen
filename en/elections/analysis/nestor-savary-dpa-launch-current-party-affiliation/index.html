Nestor, Savary and DPA: a launch did not settle every affiliation

Dated records show why appearing at a political event does not, by itself, establish someone’s eventual party affiliation.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

Le Facteur Haïti’s October 1, 2026 report on the Debout Patriotes launch named Savannah Savary among figures presented. It described Michelet Mike Nestor as a prospective presidential candidate while expressly saying selection was not yet final. The launch took place September 30 in Pétion-Ville. [1]

Kayimit News later reported DPA’s consensus selection of Nestor on November 2 and his registration on November 13. In the November 14 CEP snapshot, Nestor appears under DPA and Savary under HAMAD. Those records are later than a launch’s list of featured or prospective candidates. [2] [3]

These records do not establish that Savary left DPA following a dispute or that she and Nestor have no continuing political relationship. They do not supply every interparty agreement. They establish distinctions between appearing at an event, internal selection and the structure recorded at registration.

For voters, the distinction matters. Beyond who shares a stage, they need to know which organization mandates a candidate, which programme binds them and who can hold them answerable. Political relationships deserve examination through records extending beyond one public appearance.
