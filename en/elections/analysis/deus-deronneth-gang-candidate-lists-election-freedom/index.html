Déus Deronneth: election security begins before polling day

Reported gang-imposed candidate lists show why freedom of choice goes beyond being able to reach a polling station.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-16

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

A polling station can open after the choices leading to it have already come under pressure. If people cannot stand, criticize a list or campaign without threats, a timetable alone cannot deliver free elections.

In October 2026 Déus Deronneth sought a realistic security-restoration timetable before fixing elections, alongside government limits and results. That offers a starting point requiring measures of freedom of choice as well. [1]

Page 17 of BINUH’s July–September 2026 report describes a list allegedly prepared by Brooklyn’s gang leader to gain municipal positions in Cité Soleil. It reports two residents killed after denouncing the practice. We attribute these allegations and add no candidate names the passage does not supply. [2]

This establishes no agreement between Deronneth or another presidential candidate and the gang. It identifies a risk to the contest itself: control over who enters the choices before ballots are printed.

A readiness test should ask whether candidates and citizens can speak, assemble, complain and resist coercion. Public criteria and independent verification would help. Neither a fixed date nor unlimited postponement alone answers the freedom problem.
