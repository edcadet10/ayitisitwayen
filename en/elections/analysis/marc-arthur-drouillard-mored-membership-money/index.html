Marc Arthur Drouillard and MORED: what the $100 fee offers

A membership page combines services and political participation. The accounts need to show what each payment funds.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

On the MORED website naming Marc-Arthur Drouillard, a “1,000 Eli MORED” programme asks for $100 monthly. Advertised benefits include consultation, training, discounted Marc-Arthur services and protection rituals. These are the site’s offers, not services we have independently verified as delivered. [1]

An illustrative calculation shows why accounts matter: if 1,000 members paid $100 for one month, that would produce $100,000 before expenses. This is an example, not a receipt. We do not have evidence that this many people pay.

Another distinction matters. The page uses MORED; Drouillard’s presidential registration uses MRN. We do not declare them the same legal entity or treasury. That relationship requires organizational and financial records. [1] [2]

For a contributor, the practical questions concern who receives money, the receipt issued and the services or activities funded. When a page combines services with a political movement, separate accounts would help people understand their commitments.

This is neither a judgment on members’ beliefs nor an allegation that payments buy public decisions. It is ordinary scrutiny of an activity using a candidate’s name.
