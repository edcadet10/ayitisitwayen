Dominique Dupuy: the 2024 threats and two women on the presidential list

A registration list shows who reaches the door, not every barrier encountered.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

On March 24, 2024, Dominique Dupuy announced withdrawal from the presidential-council formation process. In the video statement reported by HaitiLibre, she described political attacks and threats to her life. She had been the RED/EDE grouping and allies’ designated representative. [1]

This is not a judgment identifying those responsible for threats. We do not assign criminal responsibility the article does not establish. Such a withdrawal statement nevertheless forms an important public part of her political record. [1]

The CEP dashboard checked November 15, 2026 shows 2 women among 32 presidential registrations: 6.25%, by our calculation. This is a snapshot of preliminary registrations, not a final list, poll or measure of candidates’ abilities. [2]

That figure alone cannot explain why other women did not register. Understanding barriers requires examining party selection, access to finance, security conditions and experiences of people who tried to run. One person’s story cannot explain the entire system.

The same scrutiny of record and programme should apply to everyone. Taking political threats seriously and examining a candidate rigorously are compatible tasks.
