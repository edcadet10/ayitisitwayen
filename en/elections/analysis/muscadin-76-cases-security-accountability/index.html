Muscadin: public protection and the 76 cases documented by the UN

Support for a security official and evidence about his methods are different questions.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

People living under gang threats want protection. In Miragoâne, several dozen demonstrators supported Jean Ernest Muscadin in November 2025, according to Vant Bèf Info. Participants portrayed the prosecutor as confronting gangs where institutions had failed to protect them. [1]

His candidacy also requires examining another record. In its April–June 2026 report, BINUH says no judicial proceedings had been initiated against Muscadin for possible involvement in at least 76 summary executions documented between 2022 and 2025: killings outside the judicial process meant to determine guilt. [2]

The dates matter. The 76 is not a death toll for the second quarter of 2026. It concerns cases from 2022–2025. A 2025 expert report had identified at least 74 cases through June that year. We do not add 74 to 76 as if they were separate groups. [2] [3]

These are allegations and observations in UN reports, not a criminal verdict. The reported absence of proceedings proves neither that the allegations are false nor that they are true. A supporting demonstration is likewise not a representative opinion poll.

The questions accompanying a security promise are concrete: who verifies the identity and conduct of people an official calls criminals? What recourse exists if there is an error? Public protection requires answers about results and limits on power.
