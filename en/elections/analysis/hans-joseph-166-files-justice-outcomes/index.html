Hans Joseph: 166 referrals, but who followed them after investigation?

An ECC inquiry shows why report counts alone cannot measure results against corruption.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

There is a distance between announcing an investigation and obtaining a court decision. In its 2025 report, watchdog Ensemble Contre la Corruption, ECC, traced 166 referrals submitted by ULCC and UCREF over 2005–2024. It located information on 95 and could not trace 71. [1]

The institutions are different: ULCC investigates corruption; UCREF handles financial intelligence. Of the 166 referrals, 96 came from ULCC and 70 from UCREF. Presidential candidate Hans Joseph is named as ULCC director in a 2021 State submission. A total spanning two agencies and almost twenty years is not the record of one term under him. [1] [2]

ECC reports court-record access problems and explanations from judicial staff including fires, relocations and armed attacks. An unlocated file does not prove someone destroyed it to conceal a crime. But an untraceable process harms both victims seeking accountability and accused people seeking resolution. [1]

A 2026 human-rights coalition submission to the UN cites the study’s result: four judgments, including two convictions. Four adjudicated files do not automatically mean four guilty people. Report counts also do not tell us how much money returned to the Treasury. [3]

Assessing Joseph requires examining ULCC’s work under his responsibility and what the courts did afterward. Assessing his promises requires a system that tracks a file from referral to final decision. A president does not replace a judge; the candidate should explain how he would support an accountable process.

    Haiti / OAS MESICIC

    State submission naming Hans Joseph as director general. The institution’s account of an investigation does not replace a court file or independent verification.

  3. Justice-sector submission for the UN human-rights review

    Haitian and Solidarity Human Rights Coalition / IJDH · 2026-07-17

    PDF page 6, paragraph 18, cites ECC’s findings of four judgments and two convictions. An NGO submission, not a UN ruling or a new audit of every file.
