Gandhy Dorfeuille: after the electricity launch, what followed?

An older project with a reported 24-million-gourde budget offers a concrete way to examine the former deputy’s experience.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

A Le Nouvelliste article published April 22, 2014 reports the launch of a second electrification phase in Saint-Louis du Sud. It names then-deputy Gandhy Dorfeuille and describes a line running from Saint-Georges to Maurisseau along National Road 2. [1]

The public excerpt reports a Treasury-funded budget of 24 million gourdes, with 18 million already available. The difference is six million. That arithmetic does not show missing money: a budget, available funds and completed expenditure are different things. [1]

When ANF announced Dorfeuille as its presidential choice in September 2026, the party emphasized his parliamentary experience. The electricity project gives that experience something measurable: a place, a service and an amount. [2]

Assessing the result would require completion records, evidence of connections entering service and responsibility for maintenance. The excerpt we read does not provide those records. That does not prove failure; it shows why a launch announcement cannot establish the outcome.

The voter’s question can be simple: how many households received electricity, for how long, and at what cost? Those answers would turn an old ceremony into an election record worth assessing.
