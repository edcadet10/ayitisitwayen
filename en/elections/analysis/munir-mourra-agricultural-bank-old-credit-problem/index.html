Munir Mourra proposes an agricultural bank. The credit problem has a history.

A new institution should explain what changes in a farmer’s life.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

The MPH manifesto carried by Munir Joseph Mourra proposes creating an agricultural bank, alongside irrigation-channel work and agricultural distribution. We are examining a party proposal, not a bank verified as already open. [1] [2]

Agricultural credit is not a new Haitian public-policy issue. In his January 2009 interview, Joanas Gué referred to the Bureau de Crédit Agricole and criticized its limited lending over ten years. That is Gué’s assessment then, not a present-day institutional balance sheet. [3]

That creates a useful comparison: is the need a new name, more capital, different guarantees or another way to assess harvest risk? These are different answers. The candidate should identify the weakness a new bank would correct.

For a farmer, credit needs to arrive before planting and repayment should fit the harvest cycle. Rules must address flooding losses, eligibility and prevention of political connections replacing sound applications.

A bank can support production without becoming a favours fund. The difference requires capital, rules and public oversight. Those would distinguish a new institution from a new sign over the same problem.
