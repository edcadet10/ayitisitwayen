Martine Moïse vs Jocelerme Privert: two decisions with different effects

Restarting an inquiry and releasing a person are different decisions. Neither automatically decides the substance of the allegations.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-16

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

When a candidate appears in proceedings, the word describing the decision matters as much as the name. In Martine Moïse’s and Jocelerme Privert’s records, a label such as cleared can erase distinctions readers need.

Vant Bèf Info reported on October 13, 2025 that the appeal court annulled the 2024 order in Jovenel Moïse’s assassination case and restarted the investigation. That is neither a judgment finding Martine Moïse guilty nor an acquittal on the merits. [1]

The June 13, 2006 Gonaïves appeal decision lifts warrants against Privert while requiring appearance when summoned. Page 3 expressly separates release from the merits of the La Scierie case. Privert rejected the allegations and claimed judicial clearance in 2009. [2] [3]

We lack complete later files establishing each proceeding’s final stage. These concern different events, years and people. Neither supplies evidence in the other; comparing procedure does not place the candidates in a shared plot.

The useful voter question is what each decision actually does: whom it releases, which act it annuls and what remains open. Those answers permit scrutiny without conviction by rumour or erasure of unresolved issues.

Sources and verification limits

  1. Appeals court restarts the Moïse assassination investigation

    Vant Bèf Info · 2025-10-13

    News report on the annulment of the earlier order and the renewed inquiry, not a certified copy of the ruling. BINUH’s reports corroborate that the supplementary investigation continued in 2026.

  2. Privert: the June 13, 2006 release decision

    Cour d’appel des Gonaïves / copy hosted by IJDH · 2006-06-13

    All three scanned pages inspected. Page 3 gives the date and operative terms, requires appearance when summoned and separates release from merits. The filename date is not the decision date. This is not presented as the final decision in the entire case.

  3. Privert rejects the La Scierie allegations during the 2009 campaign

    Scoop FM · 2009-12-18

    Reports his December 17 statement. His account of clearance is his position, not another judgment obtained by us. It does not establish that no other decision ever existed.
