Dupuy’s 33 women: the same announcement, two different tenses

The Creole text promises promotions; the French says they happened. Appointment records would resolve the timing.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-18

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

A June 24, 2024 Instagram post on Dupuy’s account announces diplomatic recalls, women’s interim leadership of more than half of missions, and recognition for 33 women ministry staff members. [1]

The Creole promotion sentence says the women will receive a promotion and be honoured. The French sentence says they have been promoted and honoured. These are different descriptions of when an action happened. [1]

A translation mismatch or an announcement written while arrangements were being completed could explain the difference. It does not establish that the promotions were invented. The appointment decisions and dates would answer the question more directly than either formulation.

There is also a counting problem to avoid. Thirty-three ministry staff members are not automatically 33 heads of mission. An interim leadership target is not a permanent staffing result. Readers need the number of missions, the appointments and the period being measured.

This review did not recover the complete appointment register or the historical staffing series needed to verify the first-ever claim. The announcement remains evidence of a stated representation agenda. Its precise timing and measured result still need documentation.
