Tardieu and Bodeau in 2019: the records, the vote and what it did not settle

A debate over impeaching Jovenel Moïse ended in a disputed vote. Its political outcome did not adjudicate every financial allegation.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

In August 2019, Jerry Tardieu urged Lower Chamber president Gary Bodeau to make requested documents available for the debate on impeaching President Jovenel Moïse. Rezo Nòdwès reported Tardieu’s account of a weekend commitment to supply them. This establishes his procedural demand, not destruction or deliberate concealment of a specific record by Bodeau. [1]

UN report S/2019/805 records the outcome: after two earlier sessions, the August 22 motion received 53 votes against, three in favour and five abstentions. It also records opposition deputies’ challenge to the vote’s legality because attendance fell below the 80-member quorum they invoked. [2]

Those totals do not identify Tardieu’s individual vote. Assigning him one of the three affirmative votes would require a named voting record. Nor was this a criminal judgment declaring every PetroCaribe transaction proper. A chamber can reject a motion while evidentiary and procedural questions remain unresolved.

For two former deputies now seeking office, the episode offers distinct tests. Tardieu can account for the records he sought and his follow-through. Bodeau can account for how his bureau organized access and debate. That requires decision records alongside public statements.
