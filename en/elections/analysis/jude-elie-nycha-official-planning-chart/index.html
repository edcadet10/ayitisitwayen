Jude Elie at NYCHA: what an official chart confirms

A 2018 document provides a specific basis for examining his housing experience.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

Jude Elie includes experience at NYCHA, New York’s public housing authority, in his online biography. A separate document on New York City’s website provides a checkable point: a 2018 organization chart for NYCHA capital planning. [1] [2]

The diagram places his name among three “A/E Planners,” under the Physical Needs Manager. We examined that placement visually. The document identifies a team role at that time; it does not establish present employment or assess his performance. [2]

This is more useful than repeating a major institution’s name without explaining the role. Housing-work planning involves identifying needs, setting priorities and fitting projects to schedules and budgets. Assessing the candidate’s experience requires his specific responsibilities.

Project records, decision responsibilities and documented outcomes would strengthen assessment. Someone can make a useful contribution without heading an agency; employment does not make every agency outcome a personal achievement.

For Haiti, the question is what that experience can bring to housing planning and maintenance. The chart starts the examination with a role and year, rather than an undefined title.
