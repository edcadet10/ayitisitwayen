Dupuy’s international network: the links the record actually shows

A fellowship list and an institutional photograph establish participation. The next reporting questions concern purpose, funding and commitments.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-18

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

Field lists Dominique Dupuy of Haiti among its 2026 fellows. The programme, founded by former New Zealand prime minister Jacinda Ardern, describes a twelve-month leadership fellowship. Dupuy’s August message thanks Ardern and the programme for the experience. [1] [2] [3]

In a separate July post, Dupuy described a conversation with OAS secretary general Albert Ramdin at Aspen. An institutional photo record places her at the July 15 Aspen event and identifies her as a former minister. The post describes the conversation; the photograph does not transcribe it. [4] [5]

These are two documented kinds of access: participation in a leadership programme and contact at an international forum. They can inform a candidate’s experience and contacts. They do not establish that the programme, Ardern, Ramdin or the OAS endorses her candidacy.

No individual funding agreement, travel-payment record, campaign donation or private-conversation transcript was recovered here. Rules now advertised for a later fellowship cohort cannot establish Dupuy’s 2026 terms. Her description of the experience as a gift also does not establish a financial gift.

The useful questions are concrete: who paid participation and travel costs, in what capacity did she attend, and did she accept commitments relevant to public office? Publish the answers and supporting records. Scrutiny should reveal how international access connects to public responsibility, without turning a meeting into an invented alliance.
