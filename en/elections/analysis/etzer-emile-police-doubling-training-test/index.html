Etzer Emile wants to double the police. When will recruits be ready?

Announcing training and putting a prepared officer on the street are different steps.

Ayiti Sitwayen · Updated: 2026-11-15

An examination of documented information, with interpretations clearly identified.

A family needs to know whether its road will be safe, not only how many people a candidate wants to recruit. Etzer Emile’s programme proposes moving from about 13,000 operational officers to 26,000 by 2033. That starting figure is the campaign’s. [1]

For the first 100 days, he promises to accelerate recruitment and training of 1,000–2,000 officers. The page also proposes recruit screening, sanctions for abuse and a stronger inspectorate. [1]

The word “accelerate” matters. It does not mean all those recruits would finish training by day 100. A timetable showing entrants, graduates and officers retained in service would let the public measure delivery.

The same recruitment total can produce two different results. If recruits replace departing officers, the force may barely grow. If they add to existing teams, more officers become available. The count must show both movements.

For someone travelling, the meaningful test is sustained protection: who pays the unit, who supervises its conduct, and does it remain after an operation? That is where a recruitment plan becomes a protection plan.
