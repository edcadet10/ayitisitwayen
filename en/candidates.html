Candidate Registration and Validation

Candidate registration data are preliminary. Online registration and document filing are separate steps. The CEP’s validated list establishes official candidacies. Consult the deadlines in the summary above and the complete calendar.

We publish candidate information based on the CEP’s officially validated lists and decisions. A name in the press or registration statistics does not establish an approved candidacy.

Requirements to Become President

A presidential candidate must meet Article 135 of the Constitution together with Article 76 of the October 6, 2026 electoral decree. Consult the CEP candidate portal for the complete requirements and supporting documents before filing.

How to Evaluate a Candidate

When the official list comes out, here are the questions to ask about each candidate: What management experience do they have? What concrete plans do they have for security, the economy, and education — and how will they pay for them? What is their record with public funds and human rights? Who is financing their campaign? Do they answer journalists' questions or only make big promises? An informed citizen does not vote for a name or a song — they vote for a plan and a record.
