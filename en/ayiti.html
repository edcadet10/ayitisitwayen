Ayiti: know the country, understand power

A country is more than the people governing it. It is communities, language, memory and citizens with rights. This page is a starting point for understanding Haiti and examining its public life.

Last reviewed: 2026-11-16

Ayiti and Haiti: the same country

Ayiti is the country’s Haitian Creole name; Haïti is its French name. Haitian Creole and French are official languages. Port-au-Prince is the capital. Haiti shares Hispaniola with the Dominican Republic and gained independence from France in 1804. The source for these names and facts appears below. [1]
